# Table tools: the roll presentation and the partner tab (T52)

Sam's review: "Dice and coin rolls need a presentation moment. Instantly rolling when pressing the
option is too anticlimactic." The presentation lives on the dice panel only (per-seat dice and coin
effects were dropped: "impossible to tell which player is acting"), in held keyframes (SPEC rule 4),
on both faces of the panel (the upper face is the lower one turned 180°, so every side of the table
reads it).

Sheets (4×, every keyframe left to right, the settled result last, drawn by the real panel at S = 4):
`design/captures/t52-dice-presentation.png`, `design/captures/t52-coin-presentation.png`.
Device keyframes: `design/captures/t52-d20-*.png`, `t52-coin-*.png` (`tools/capture_t52.sh`, which
launches with `--ef diceHold 16` so each screencap lands inside its keyframe).

## Variant A: "tumble across the face" (built)

The die enters at the far end of each face and travels towards the result box in three held poses,
then lands there. The solids are real 3-D models (an icosahedron, a cube, a 24-sided coin) turned to
each pose and projected, facets shaded by a light from the upper left in paper / D4 / D8 with 1-px ink
edges (`author.py`). **No digits or pips** appear before the landing: the result is fixed at the tap
but never readable early. Label and quip are held back until the result settles.

| # | die (d20 / d6) | coin | hold |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 toss | pose A at the far end, high, speed ticks | tilted face up, spin arcs above and below | 250 ms |
| 2 bounce | pose B mid-face, on the floor, dust puffs | edge-on at the top of its arc | 250 ms |
| 3 skid | pose C beside the result box, speed ticks | the other face, low, speed ticks | 250 ms |
| 4 land | the result **stamped**: paper numerals on an ink plate, impact ticks | the coin face, impact ticks | 300 ms |
| result | plain numerals, label, quip | coin, HEADS / TAILS, quip | — |

Character in the landing (beat 4):
- **natural 20**: the stamp, impact ticks and four twinkles across the face (held 400 ms);
- **natural 1**: no stamp — the 1 just flops there with a sweat drop and a sad little puff (400 ms);
- **heads**: a glint on the coin's rim; **tails**: lands with a thunk (a dust puff and two thud lines).

Totals 1050 ms (d20, d6, coin), 1150 ms (naturals): five panel redraws per roll.

**Fast display (T59, `eink/DisplaySpeed` FAST: the Supernote's fast view mode, or a screen that isn't
e-ink):** the same path in **five** tumbling poses held 150 ms each — A, the in-between AB, B on the
floor with the dust, BC, C beside the box (the in-betweens are the solids turned halfway, from
`author.py`; the coin: face, tilting, edge, tilting back, the other face) — then the same landing. The
same 1050 / 1150 ms, seven redraws. A slow display (e-ink on the normal waveform) keeps exactly the
table above. The speed is read at each roll; both presentations share one sprite table (six more
sprites, prewarmed with the rest). A tap anywhere on the
panel during it (but ×, which still closes) jumps to the result; AGAIN rolls and presents again. One
frame timer, armed only while a presentation plays; pausing the app shows the result and leaves
nothing armed. Sprites: `DiceSprites.kt` (generated by `author.py`); frame scripts:
`tools/Presentation.kt`.

## The empty panel (T59)

"Opening the die menu should not immediately select D20 — start with empty." The gem's DICE opens the
panel with nothing rolled and nothing chosen: each face shows the result box outlined in dots and
"dice" over "Pick your poison"; no fold button is solid; the roll-again diamond is not drawn (the
dotted fold runs through the crossing) and its spot takes no tap. The first pick rolls and presents.

## Variant B: "dust-up on the spot" (not built)

The die never travels: a cartoon fight cloud (D4, inked outline) boils on the result box with a
corner of the die and a "?" poking out — two poses, 300 ms each — then the cloud is gone and the
result is stamped where it was (350 ms; the naturals as in A). The coin spins on the spot: face,
edge, the other face (250 ms each), then lands. Kept as test-only data (`DiceVariantB.kt`,
`DiceSpritesB.kt`) so the sheet stays drawable. Why A: the cloud flips a large dither area three
times in the same place (more ghosting risk on the panel, and it hides the box the result lands in),
and "travel then land" reads as a throw from across the table; B's still-life rattle reads as
waiting. B is cheaper in time (950 ms) if A feels long on the Nomad.

## First player: the d20 roll-off (T59)

"Re-imagine first player as simultaneous d20 rolls — take over the player tiles for the animation, then
life total digits are repurposed for the result." The FIRST facet makes every live seat roll a d20 at
once, on its own tile (KO'd seats sit it out):

| beat | every rolling tile | hold |
|---|---|---|
| take-over | the life total, ±, peeks, badges, drawer, readout, effects and turn tab give way (a cover); the tile gets a **double ink frame** and "D20" where the readout sits — unmistakably not a life total | — |
| tumble | the dice panel's d20 tumbling across the tile, scaled to two thirds of the hero numerals and turned with the seat; each seat starts on a different pose | slow 3 × 250 ms, fast 5 × 150 ms |
| landed | every roll in the hero numerals, where the life total was; the highest gets the **FIRST flag** — an ink pennant (notched ends) in the under-number row with the line, e.g. "ON THE PLAY" | 1600 ms |
| tie | the tied seats' bands outlined in dots with "TIE!", then only they roll again (as above); at most 3 rounds, then a random pick among them | 700 ms |
| back | the tiles return to their life totals; turn tracking starts from the winner (one undo step); T55's `start-flag` effect plays on the winner's tile as the **sting**; the FIRST flag stays on | 4 s, or the next touch |

A tap during the tumble lands every roll at once; a tap on the landing goes straight back; pause
finishes it. The "ME?" beat and the old badge are gone (the flag replaces the badge). The first-player
lines were retuned to say who starts: "On the play", "First out the gates", "You lead off", "Kick us
off", "Top of the order", "Floor's yours", "Opening act", "Turn one is yours", "Set the pace", "Lead the
charge". Code: `tools/FirstRoll` (state), `tools/TileRollLayout` (geometry), `tools/FirstPlayer`.

## Partner tab (commander-damage mode)

"Unclear how to set a player to have partners." In commander mode every tile's drawer-tab spot (the
near edge, facing its player — tabs are hidden in the mode) becomes that player's **PARTNER** tab. One
control per player rather than per quarter: it is the same spot at every count (2–6), every tier and
on sideways seats, and a player can set their own partner even while they are the target. Press =
inversion, action on release.

Look (T59, "use the dither ground … a halo/tape … the little partner and a plus or minus"): the drawer
tab's dither ground (`Role.TAB`); the sidekick icon on its 1-px contour halo — `partner-add` (+) when
the seat has no partner, `partner-remove` (−) when it has one — and the word PARTNER on a **tape**
(the SPEC's paper strip for title words on the tab checker). On vs off at a glance beyond the sign:
the tape is paper with ink letters when off, **ink with paper letters when on**. Icon options (A
built: the sidekick with a sign at its shoulder; B: the sign knocked out of a solid badge) in
`design/captures/t59-partner-icons.png`; B's files are in `design/icons/candidates/t59/`. The word is
dropped where it does not fit beside the icon, the icon where the tab is shorter than it (S = 6).

Turning partner **off** zeroes the partner's damage dealt and its tax in core (life is not refunded).
With nothing recorded a tap does it; with something recorded a tap only says **HOLD** on the tape for
2.5 s and holding the tab (500 ms, the first hold repeat) drops it. Every change is one `SetPartner`:
one undo step, logged in the game history (`LogEvent.PartnerChanged`).
